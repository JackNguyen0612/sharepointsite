The Challenge

Is Your Pricing Holding Your Firm Back?

Many financial planning firms face common pricing challenges that limit growth and profitability.

⚠

One-Size-Fits-All Pricing

A rigid pricing model that doesn't flex to cover the diverse needs and complexities of different client profiles, leaving revenue on the table.

🔍

Unclear Value Communication

Advisers struggle to articulate and defend the true value of their advice, making fee conversations with clients uncomfortable and inconsistent.

📊

No Pricing Visibility

Without a system to track upfront and ongoing fee arrangements, firms lack the insight needed to manage revenue and measure progress effectively.

What is iDELIVER?

iDELIVER is Peloton Partners' specialist pricing and implementation program designed exclusively for wealth management and financial planning firms.

We work alongside your team to develop a new pricing framework that accurately reflects the value and services your firm delivers to every client — both new and existing.

  • ✓ Customised pricing framework for upfront and ongoing fees tailored to your firm
  • ✓ Client communication strategy and presentation documents developed for you
  • ✓ Adviser coaching on positioning and defending the value of your advice
  • ✓ Exclusive access to yourLeadOut software to manage and track all client pricing
  • ✓ Weekly progress reporting and ongoing accountability support

Fair & Personalised

Every client receives a fee that is unique to their individual circumstances, goals and complexity level — because no two clients are the same.

Evidence-Based

An underlying set of questions and considerations for each service delivered provides a robust evidence base when calculating client fees.

Team Conviction

Advisers and the team build genuine conviction and belief in the value they deliver, leading to confident and consistent client conversations.

The Process

How iDELIVER Works

A carefully staged 2–3 month process that plans every element before advisers begin transitioning clients to the new pricing framework.

1

Discovery & Data Collection

We meet with key management and advice staff to understand your team, services and current pricing. A confidential staff survey captures individual feedback on value, time and services delivered across all client support roles.

Month 1
2

Pricing Framework Development

We analyse active client data and build a recommended pricing framework for upfront and ongoing fees. The framework is presented to advisers along with training on the client mapping process to stress-test the new models.

Month 1–2
3

Testing & Refinement

Advisers sample-test a group of clients in yourLeadOut to identify individual complexities. We finalise the service and pricing framework and develop the client communication strategy document.

Month 2
4

Coaching & Training

We present the final pricing framework and conduct coaching sessions — both group and individual role-play — to train advisers on articulating value and handling objections. Full training on yourLeadOut software is also provided.

Month 2–3
5

Go Live & Ongoing Support

Advisers begin meeting existing clients to present the new fee arrangements. Peloton provides regular meetings, coaching and weekly update reports tracking progress, completed transitions and revenue uplift achieved.
